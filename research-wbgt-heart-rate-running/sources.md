# Sources — wbgt-heart-rate-running

_Locked, authoritative references. Every piece artifact cites these by index [n] and adds new items here._

[1] ACGIH. _Occupational and Environmental Heat Stress Evaluation Criteria_ (2023). WBGT definition and formula components.
    URL: https://www.acgih.org/

[2] ISO 7243:2021. _Ergonomics of the thermal environment — Analytical determination and interpretation of thermal comfort and heat stress_ (Appendix A: WBGT).
    URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/79366.html

[3] Westerholm, G. "Heat stress in athletes." _Sports Medicine_, 1988;5(3):165-179. (Cited by ACSM [8] as the basis for the thermoregulatory skin-blood-flow mechanism.)
    URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Westerholm+heat+stress+athletes+1988  (NOTE: the originally listed PMID 3291093 is an unrelated prostate-cancer paper; corrected to a search link.)

[4] CDC / NIOSH. _Criteria for a Recommended Standard: Occupational Exposure to Heat and Hot Environments_. DHHS (NIOSH) Publication 2016-106 (Feb 2016).
    URL: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/publications/numbered/2016-106.html  (NOTE: originally listed as 2016-111, which is the unrelated diacetyl publication; corrected to 2016-106.)

[5] OpenWeatherMap API: Current, 5-day/3-hour, One Call API. Free tier.
    URL: https://openweathermap.org/api

[6] Open-Meteo API: Forecast API with radiation, cloud cover, temperature, humidity, wind. Free, no API key.
    URL: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs

[7] NOAA Solar Calculator / SPA. Approximate solar radiation from time + latitude/longitude.
    URL: https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/

[8] American College of Sports Medicine. _Exertional Heat Illness during Training and Competition_. Position Stand. Med Sci Sports Exerc 2007;39(3):556-572. DOI 10.1249/MSS.0b013e31802fa199.
    URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17473783/  (NOTE: originally listed as a 2018 stand; the exertional-heat-illness position stand is the 2007 version above.)

[9] Romps, D.M. et al. "A 1-minute-resolution dataset of global weather and climate over 1998–2020 using ERA5." _Nature Sci Data_, 2022.
    URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01295-w

[10] WMO. _Guide to Meteorological Instruments and Methods of Observation_ (CIMO Guide). Radiation measurement standards.
    URL: https://public.wmo.int/en/resource-library/cimo-guide

[11] Katica, C. P. et al. "Heat strain differences walking in hot-dry and warm-wet environments of equivalent wet bulb globe temperature." _Int J Environ Res Public Health_ (2024). At a fixed 60% HRmax effort, heart rate remained ~5–10 bpm higher in hot-dry vs. warm-wet conditions at equal WBGT.
    URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11583583/
